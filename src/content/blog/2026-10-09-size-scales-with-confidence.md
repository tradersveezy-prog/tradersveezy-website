People hear “risk first” and think every trade must burn the same dollar amount. That’s not how I run it.

The **stop** still decides the geometry. Entry, invalidation, target — then R:R. Under about 1 : 2, I usually pass.

What changes is **how much of the account** I put behind that geometry.

## Confidence is not vibes

It’s a stack of yes/no:

- Can I name the regime on context (12H / 6H)?
- Is the level real (value, prior defense), not a random line?
- Do I have the trigger — or am I early?
- Cross-asset: is BTC allowing the idea?

More yes → I can take fuller risk. Gaps in the stack → smaller, or flat.

## What I refuse

- Sizing up because I’m bored or tilting
- Full size on a transition I’m still naming
- Ignoring the stop so the “size” looks bigger

Process is fixed. Size is earned.
