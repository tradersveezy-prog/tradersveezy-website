# 1.5 — Multi-timeframe without overthinking

**Status:** Locked to your dump  
**Time:** ~10 min read

---

## Chart setup

| Role | Timeframe | Job |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Context | **12H** and **6H** | Condition, bias, key levels, value |
| Entry | **15m** | Triggers, management, the closes you wait for |

Two context charts, one entry chart. That is enough.

## How to use it

1. On **12H / 6H**: trend (HH/HL + EMAs), value/range, or flip?  
2. On **15m**: does the trigger (VAL reclaim, break + close back, with-trend continuation) actually print?  
3. If the name is not Bitcoin: check **Bitcoin** before you size up — especially on a flip.

## Defaults

| Situation | Default |
|-----------|---------|
| Clean trend on context | Do not freeze for perfect 15m fills |
| Value / range on context | Do not trade mid-value on 15m |
| Break / unclear reclaim | Several 15m closes + Bitcoin check; no rush |

## Teach this

> I read **12H and 6H** for what the market is doing. I enter on **15m**. If I cannot name trend, value/range, or a flip on the higher charts, I do not invent it on the 15. On a flip I wait for more than one close, and I check Bitcoin when I am in an alt.

## Common mistakes

- Ignoring 12/6 and trading every 15m pattern  
- Renaming the condition on one 15m candle after a break  
- Skipping the Bitcoin check because “this pair looks fine”
