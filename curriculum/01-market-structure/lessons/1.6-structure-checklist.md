# 1.6 — Structure checklist

**Status:** Locked to your dump  
**Use:** Before levels deep-dive and triggers. Print or keep in notes.

---

## Pre-trade gate (structure only)

Yes / no. Any **no** → wait or skip.

1. I named the condition: **trend / range (value) / flip**.  
2. My why matches the playbook:  
   - Trend → HH/HL + above (or below) **21, 55 & 200 EMA** on 15m  
   - Range → **value / VAL reclaim** — not the middle  
   - Flip → **break + clear close back in** + volume/RSI confirm  
3. Context is on **12H & 6H**; entry plan is on **15m**.  
4. Avoid list checked:  
   - Trend → I am not frozen waiting for a perfect fill  
   - Range → I am not trading the middle  
   - Flip → I am not rushing; multi-close + Bitcoin check if needed  
5. I am thinking **buyers vs sellers** and value (POC / premium-discount / acceptance), not random lines.  
6. The plan is thought out enough that I can say: *You can't regret a well thought out plan.*

## What I do not do here

| Condition | I do not… |
|-----------|-----------|

| Range | Trade the middle |
| Flip | Rush — I wait for several closes + Bitcoin confirmation |
