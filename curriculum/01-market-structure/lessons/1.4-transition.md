# 1.4 — Flip (transition)

**Status:** Locked to your dump  
**Time:** ~12 min read

---

## Definition

**Flip / failed break:**

1. Price **breaks past a key level**  
2. Then prints a **clear close back inside**  
3. Confirmed with **volume** and **RSI** supporting the reclaim

This is not “one wick and I am in.” Break + **close back** + confirmation.

## Buyers / sellers

A break past a key level often traps one side or runs stops. The close back inside says the break did not get accepted. Volume and RSI check that the rejection has weight, not just a thin wick.

## What to avoid

**Do not rush.**

Wait for:

- **Several candle closes** (not a single emotional bar)  
- **Bitcoin confirmation** when the name is not Bitcoin itself  

No confirmation → stay flat or tiny. Structure’s job here is permission to wait.

## Failed break vs real regime change

| Failed break (flip tell) | Real new trend |
|--------------------------|----------------|
| Key level broken, close back inside, confirms | Acceptance holds beyond the level; HH/HL + EMAs start to fit 1.2 |
| Patience + multi-close + Bitcoin check | Do not force the old range label forever — but do not rename on one candle |
