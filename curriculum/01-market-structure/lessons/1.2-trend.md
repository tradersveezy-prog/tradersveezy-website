# 1.2 — Trend

**Status:** Locked to your dump  
**Time:** ~12 min read

---

## Your definition

**Trend = higher highs / higher lows, and price above the 55 EMA and 200 EMA** (bull).  
Mirror for bear: lower highs / lower lows, below those EMAs.

Both parts matter: the swing structure *and* the EMA location. HH/HL under a broken EMA stack is not the clean trend you want to chase as “trend day.”

## How to read it (buyers / sellers)

In a bull trend, buyers are defending higher lows and keeping acceptance above the EMA stack. Pullbacks are pauses — sellers get absorbed, then the next impulse makes a higher high.

You’re not counting candle colors. You’re asking: *are buyers still in control on structure and on the EMAs?*

## What you refuse in trend

**You don’t wait for the “exact” textbook entry.**

A truly trending environment often **doesn’t give** the perfect touch. If you only take the surgical fill, you miss the move you already correctly labeled.

Practical read:

- Label trend clean → look for *with*-trend participation (Course 03 owns the trigger).
- Don’t freeze waiting for a perfect wick that never prints.

## Trade / wait / skip

| Read | Default |
|------|---------|
| HH/HL + above 55 & 200 | Bias with trend; don’t demand perfection |
| HH/HL but fighting the EMAs | Not your clean trend — reassess |
| Impulses shortening, EMAs flattening | Tired — stand down or wait for 1.4 |

## Worksheet

Three charts: clean trend / tired trend / “looks like HHHL but wrong side of EMAs.” One sentence each.
