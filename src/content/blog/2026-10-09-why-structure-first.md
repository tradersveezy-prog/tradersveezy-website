Before you hunt a level or a trigger, name what the chart is doing.

Candles are **buyers and sellers**, not decoration around lines. If you can’t say trend, range (value), or transition in plain language, you’re guessing — and guessing is how people take every signal and call it process.

## The stack I actually use

- **Context:** 12H and 6H — regime and critical levels
- **Execution:** 15m — triggers and the closes you wait for

If those fight each other, don’t invent a story. Note the conflict and either wait or size like you’re wrong.

## Three labels

**Trend** — higher highs / higher lows, and price on the right side of the 55 and 200 EMA. In a clean trend I don’t freeze for the perfect fill. Real trends often won’t give it.

**Range** — value work. Reclaim of the value area low, not boredom in the middle. Mid-value chopping is where people invent trades.

**Transition** — sweep past a critical level, clear close back inside, volume and RSI with the reclaim. I don’t rush. Multiple closes. BTC / cross-asset when I’m in an alt.

## The line I repeat

You can’t regret a well thought out plan.

Structure is the first part of that plan. Entries without a regime label aren’t a plan.
