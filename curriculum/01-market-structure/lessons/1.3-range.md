# 1.3 — Range

**Status:** Locked to your dump  
**Time:** ~12 min read

---

## Definition

Range work here is **value work** — volume profile, not a random box.

The concrete signal:

**Reclaim of the Value Area Low (VAL)** on the volume profile.

That reclaim says value is being defended again after a dip through or under VAL. The “range” is not “draw two lines and trade the middle.” It is where value lives and whether price is accepted back into it.

(Course 02 goes deeper on VAH / VAL / POC. Here you only need: range = value context.)

## Buyers / sellers

A VAL reclaim is buyers stepping in where the auction previously found the low end of value. Sellers who pressed below get answered. Thinking in sides beats drawing lines without a reason.

Same mindset applies to:

- POC / fair value  
- Premium vs discount relative to value  
- Acceptance inside value vs rejection / flip  

## What to avoid in range

**Do not trade the middle.**

Edges of value (and reclaim behavior) matter. Mid-value chopping is where boredom invents trades.

## Trade / wait / skip

| Read | Default |
|------|---------|
| Clear value + VAL reclaim idea | Look for the trigger at that behavior (Course 03) |
| Price stuck mid-value, no reclaim story | Skip |
| Value unclear / profile messy | Wait — do not invent a box |

## Worksheet

Mark VAL (and POC if obvious). Note whether price reclaimed VAL with acceptance — or just wicked through. One sentence: trade / wait / skip.
