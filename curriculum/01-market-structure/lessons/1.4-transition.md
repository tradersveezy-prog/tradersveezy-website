# 1.4 — Transition

**Status:** Locked to your dump  
**Time:** ~12 min read

---

## Your definition

**Transition / change of character (failed break / sweep):**

1. Price **sweeps past a critical level**  
2. Then prints a **clear close back inside the range**  
3. Confirmed with **volume** and **RSI** reading with the reclaim (your example: bearish volume + RSI when the sweep fails back down / back in)

This is not “one wick and I’m in.” Sweep + **close back** + confirmation.

## Buyers / sellers

A sweep past a critical level is one side getting trapped or liquidity getting run. The close back inside says the break didn’t get acceptance — the auction rejected the excursion. Volume and RSI are your check that the rejection has weight, not just a thin wick.

## What you refuse in transition

**You don’t rush.**

You wait for:

- **Multiple candle closes** (not a single emotional bar)  
- **BTC pair / cross-asset confirmation** when the name isn’t BTC itself  

No confirmation stack → you stay flat or tiny. Structure’s job here is permission to wait.

## Failed break vs real regime change

| Your sweep + close back (transition tell) | Real new trend |
|-------------------------------------------|----------------|
| Critical level swept, close back inside, confirms | Acceptance *holds* beyond the level; HH/HL + EMA stack start to fit 1.2 |
| Patience + multi-close + cross-asset | Don’t force the old range label forever — but don’t rename on one candle |
