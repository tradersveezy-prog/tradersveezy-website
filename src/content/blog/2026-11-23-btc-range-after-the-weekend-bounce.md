That was a strong reversal after a bloody week. Now I want to see how much of it we can hold once the weekday flows return.

The bounce happened over the weekend, with U.S. spot ETF share trading closed. My guess is Asia played a decent part, though the chart alone can’t tell us who was buying. What matters now is whether that demand carries through into the week.

I’ve mapped out golden pockets in both directions, overlapping with naked value levels. There’s liquidity on either side, and I wouldn’t be surprised if we take both before a cleaner trend develops. A push up gets everyone bullish, a flush down gets everyone bearish, and anyone chasing gets chopped up.

The upper area I’m watching is roughly **$62.5K–$64.1K**. That’s the upper golden pocket and an area where I’d look for a reaction after a run higher. If we take nearby highs into that zone, I want to see whether buyers can hold it or whether we reject back below.

Below, I’ve got **$56.6K–$57.8K**, the lower golden pocket. Same idea there. A sweep into that area followed by a reclaim would get my attention for a long.

In between, there’s the imbalance around **$59.1K–$59.4K**. That could act as a short-term magnet as price rotates through this range. I’d use it as somewhere to take profit or reassess, especially if I’m trading from one of the outer zones.

My base case is that we spend some time going back and forth. We’ve had a big impulse, but I want to see acceptance outside these areas before I start treating every move as the next trend.

For now, I’m happy playing the range. If we reject the upper zone, I’ll look toward the middle first. If we sweep and reclaim the lower zone, same thing in reverse. If we break out and hold the retest, I’ll adjust.

> Don’t overcomplicate it. Draw your levels, set alerts, and let price come to you. Then drop to the 5m or 15m and wait for a confirmed MSS with increased volume behind the break.

There’s plenty of room between these levels. I don’t need to catch the exact top or bottom to get a decent trade.
