# Course 01 — Market Structure

**Tag:** Foundations  
**Status:** Locked to `YOUR-DUMP.md` (2026-10-09)  
**One-liner:** Trend, range, and flip. Read what buyers and sellers are doing before you look for a trade.

## Locked definitions (from dump)

| Condition | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Trend** | HH/HL + above 21 / 55 / 200 EMA on 15m |
| **Range** | Value work — reclaim of VAL; no middle |
| **Flip** | Break past key level + clear close back inside + volume/RSI confirm |
| **TFs** | Entry **15m** · Context **12H & 6H** |
| **Phrase** | You can't regret a well thought out plan. |

### Avoid

- Trend → do not wait for exact entries (trend often will not give them)
- Range → do not trade the middle
- Flip → do not rush; several closes + Bitcoin confirmation

### Teaching premise

Candles = buyers & sellers. Tie structure to POCs, fair value, premium/discount, value acceptance/flips — not complication for its own sake.
