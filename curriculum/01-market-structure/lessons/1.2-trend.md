# 1.2 — Trend

**Status:** Locked to your dump  
**Time:** ~12 min read

---

## Definition

**Trend = higher highs / higher lows, and price above the 21, 55, and 200 EMAs** on the 15-minute (bull).  
Mirror for bear: lower highs / lower lows, below those EMAs.

Both parts matter: the swing structure and the EMA location. Higher highs under a broken set of averages is not the clean trend you want.

## How to read it

In a bull trend, buyers defend higher lows and keep price accepted above those averages. Pullbacks are pauses — sellers get absorbed, then the next move makes a higher high.

You are not counting candle colors. Ask: are buyers still in control on structure and on the EMAs?

## What to avoid in trend

**Do not wait forever for a perfect textbook entry.**

A strong trend often does not give one. If you only take the surgical fill, you miss the move you already labeled correctly.

Practical read:

- Clean trend → look for with-trend participation (Course 03 owns the trigger).
- Do not freeze waiting for a perfect wick that never prints.

## Trade / wait / skip

| Read | Default |
|------|---------|
| HH/HL + above 21, 55 & 200 on 15m | Bias with trend; do not demand perfection |
| HH/HL but fighting the EMAs | Not a clean trend — reassess |
| Impulses shortening, EMAs flattening | Tired — stay flat or wait for 1.4 |

## Worksheet

Three charts: clean trend / tired trend / “looks like HHHL but wrong side of EMAs.” One sentence each.
