# 1.1 — Why structure first

**Status:** Locked to your dump (2026-10-09)  
**Time:** ~10 min read

---

Before you hunt a level or a trigger, name what the chart is doing.

If you can’t say **trend**, **range**, or **transition** in one sentence, you’re guessing.

## The premise (how you teach)

Candles are **buyers and sellers**, not decoration around lines. Structure is the story of who is in control — then you tie that story to value (POC, fair value, premium/discount, value acceptance or flips). Random lines without that thought process is noise.

Complication that only sounds technical gets cut.

## The rule

**If I can’t name the regime, I’m not in.**

Waiting is a position.
