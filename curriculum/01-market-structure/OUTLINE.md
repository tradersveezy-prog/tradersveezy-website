# Course 01 — Market Structure

**Tag:** Foundations  
**Status:** Locked to `YOUR-DUMP.md` (2026-10-09)  
**One-liner:** Trend, range and transition. How to read what the chart is actually doing before you look for a trade.

## Locked definitions (from dump)

| Regime | Your rule |
|--------|-----------|
| **Trend** | HH/HL + above 55 EMA / 200 EMA |
| **Range** | Value work — reclaim of VAL (volume profile); no middle |
| **Transition** | Sweep past critical level + clear close back inside + volume/RSI confirm |
| **TFs** | Execution **15m** · Context **12H & 6H** |
| **Phrase** | You can't regret a well thought out plan. |

### Refusals

- Trend → don’t wait for exact entries (trend won’t give them)
- Range → don’t trade the middle
- Transition → don’t rush; multiple closes + BTC/cross-asset confirmation

### Teaching premise

Candles = buyers & sellers. Tie structure to POCs, fair value, premium/discount, value acceptance/flips — not complication for its own sake.
