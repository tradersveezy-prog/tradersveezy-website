# 2.3 — Volume profile in plain words

- **POC** — where the most volume traded.
- **VAL** — low end of value. A range long often needs a reclaim here.
- **VAH** — high end of value. Where acceptance gets tested.

“Premium” and “discount” describe expensive vs cheap relative to value — the same idea as VAH and VAL.

**Avoid:** Trading the middle of value with no clear reason.
