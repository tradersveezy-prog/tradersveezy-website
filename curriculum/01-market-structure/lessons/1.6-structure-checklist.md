# 1.6 — Structure checklist (worksheet)

**Status:** Locked to your dump  
**Use:** Before levels deep-dive and triggers. Print or keep in notes.

---

## Pre-trade gate (structure only)

Yes / no. Any **no** → wait or skip.

1. I named the regime: **trend / range (value) / transition**.  
2. My why matches the playbook:  
   - Trend → HH/HL + above (or below) **55 & 200 EMA**  
   - Range → **value / VAL reclaim** story — not mid-box boredom  
   - Transition → **sweep + clear close back in** + volume/RSI confirm  
3. Context is on **12H & 6H**; execution plan is on **15m**.  
4. Regime refusal checked:  
   - Trend → I’m not frozen waiting for a perfect fill  
   - Range → I’m not trading the middle  
   - Transition → I’m not rushing; multi-close + BTC/cross-asset if needed  
5. I’m thinking **buyers vs sellers** and value (POC / premium-discount / acceptance), not random lines.  
6. The plan is thought out enough that I can say: *You can't regret a well thought out plan.*
