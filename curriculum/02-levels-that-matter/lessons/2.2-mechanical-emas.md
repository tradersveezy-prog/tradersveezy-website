# 2.2 — 55 & 200 EMA as levels

The **55 EMA** and **200 EMA** are not only trend filters.

Orders are often placed at those averages, so price frequently reacts there. Treat them as real levels.

**How to use them**

- Mark them the same way you mark a clear horizontal on your context charts.
- A tag and hold (especially with a wick) is worth noting.
- Stronger when they sit inside a buy or sell zone you already care about.
- Still wait for your entry process — do not buy the first touch blindly.

**Avoid:** Ignoring them because “moving averages are not real levels.” Many orders treat them as real.
