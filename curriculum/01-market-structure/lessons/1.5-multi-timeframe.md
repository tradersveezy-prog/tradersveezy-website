# 1.5 — Multi-timeframe without overthinking

**Status:** Locked to your dump  
**Time:** ~10 min read

---

## Your stack

| Role | Timeframe | Job |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Context | **12H** and **6H** | Regime, bias, critical levels, value context |
| Execution | **15m** | Triggers, management, the closes you wait for |

Two context clocks, one execution clock. That’s enough.

## How to use it

1. On **12H / 6H**: trend (HH/HL + 55/200), value/range, or transition?  
2. On **15m**: does the trigger (VAL reclaim, sweep+close back, with-trend continuation) actually print?  
3. If the name isn’t BTC: check **BTC / cross-asset** before you size up — especially in transition.

## Conflict rules (from your refusals)

| Situation | Your default |
|-----------|----------------|
| Clean trend on context | Don’t freeze for perfect 15m fills |
| Value / range on context | Don’t trade mid-value on 15m |
| Sweep / unclear break | Multiple 15m closes + cross-asset; no rush |

## Beginner stack (teach this)

> I read **12H and 6H** for what the market is doing. I execute on **15m**. If I can’t name trend, value/range, or a sweep transition on the higher TFs, I don’t invent it on the 15. In transition I wait for more than one close, and I check BTC when I’m in an alt.

## Anti-patterns

- Ignoring 12/6 and trading every 15m pattern  
- Renaming regime on one 15m candle after a sweep  
- Skipping cross-asset confirmation because “this pair looks fine”
