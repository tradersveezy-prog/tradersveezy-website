## In your own words

### How do you define TREND?

higher highs/lows, above the 55 ema/200ema

### How do you define RANGE?

reclaim of value area low of volume profile

### How do you define TRANSITION / change of character?

sweep past a critical level and a clear close back inside the range, with bearish volume and rsi reading confirming.

### What you refuse to do in each regime

- In trend I don’t: wait for exact entries, truly trending enviroment truly give you that entry
- In range I don’t: trade in the middle
- In transition I don’t: rush, i wait for multiple candle closes and btc pair cross asset confirmation

### Anything you hate seeing in “structure” courses

Complication created just to sound smart or techincal. I work on the premis that candles are buyer and sellers, not just lines. Once we emphasis the thought process of thinking like each side and tie that in to pocs, and fair values, extensions, premiums nad discounts, value range acceptance or flips, we can better think about context and not random lines on a chart.
