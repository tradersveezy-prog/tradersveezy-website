## Your definition (how you actually use “range”)

Range work for you is **value work** — volume profile, not a random box.

The concrete tell you named:

**Reclaim of the Value Area Low (VAL)** on the volume profile.

That reclaim is the structure signal that value is being defended / accepted again after a dip through or under VAL. The “range” isn’t “draw two lines and scalp the middle.” It’s *where value lives* and whether price is accepted back into it.

(Course 02 goes deeper on VAH / VAL / POC. Here you only need: range = value context.)

## Buyers / sellers

A VAL reclaim is buyers stepping in where the auction previously found the low end of value. Sellers who pressed below get answered. Thinking in sides beats drawing lines for sport.

Tie the same mindset to:

- POC / fair value  
- Premium vs discount relative to value  
- Acceptance inside value vs rejection / flip  

## What you refuse in range

**You don’t trade the middle.**

Edge of value (and reclaim/acceptance behavior) matters. Mid-value chopping is where boredom invents trades.

## Trade / wait / skip

| Read | Default |
|------|---------|
| Clear value + VAL reclaim thesis | Look for the trigger at that behavior (Course 03) |
| Price stuck mid-value, no reclaim story | Skip |
| Value unclear / profile messy | Wait — don’t invent a box |

## Worksheet

Mark VAL (and POC if obvious). Note whether price reclaimed VAL with acceptance — or just wicked through. One sentence: trade / wait / skip.
