# 1.1 — Why structure first

**Status:** Locked to your dump (2026-10-09)  
**Time:** ~10 min read

---

Before you hunt a level or a trigger, name what the chart is doing.

If you cannot say **trend**, **range**, or **flip** in one sentence, you are guessing.

## The premise

Candles are **buyers and sellers**, not decoration around lines. Structure is who is in control — then you tie that to value (POC, fair value, premium/discount, acceptance or flips). Lines without that thinking are noise.

Extra complication that only sounds technical gets cut.

## The rule

**If I cannot name the condition, I am not in.**

Waiting is a valid choice.

## Chart setup (day trade)

| Role | TF |
|------|-----|
| Entry | **15m** |
| Context | **12H and 6H** |

Name the condition on context first, then see if 15m agrees enough to act. Details in 1.5.

## Phrase you use

> You can't regret a well thought out plan.

Structure is the first part of that plan. Entries without a named condition are not a plan.
