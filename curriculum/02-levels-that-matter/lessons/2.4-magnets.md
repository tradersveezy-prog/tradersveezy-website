# 2.4 — Price magnets

Some prints leave a mark. Price often returns to them.

## Wide flat pair

Not one doji. **Two wide bodies** sharing a flat edge:

1. A wide candle **closes flat** into a level (little or no wick past that edge)  
2. The next wide candle **opens from the same flat**

Mark that shared open/close. Price often revisits it later (see AAVE ~160 on 12H).

## Long wick · 0.5

Mark the extreme of a long wick. Also mark the **midpoint (0.5)** of that wick. On the way back, price often reacts at the halfway point — not only at the tip.

## VWAP

**VWAP** is the volume-weighted average price for the session — fair value for the day. Stretches away often pull back toward it. Treat a tag and hold like any other level; still wait for your entry process.

**Avoid:** Treating every candle as a magnet. Use obvious prints only.
